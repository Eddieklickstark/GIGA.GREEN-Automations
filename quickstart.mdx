## Overview

Vantum helps you evaluate commercial roof spaces for solar energy projects. You can create an account, submit site details, and receive energy production estimates within minutes.

<Callout kind="info">
  Before you begin, prepare your company details including address, number of locations, and basic roof information. Large corporations should have data for site networks ready.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

You need the following to complete setup:

- Valid business email address
- Company registration details
- Approximate roof area measurements per location
- Contact information for site managers

## Create Your Account

Follow these steps to set up your Vantum profile.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up" icon="user-plus">
    Visit the registration page and enter your business email. Confirm your account via the verification link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete company profile" icon="building">
    Add your company name, address, and industry type. Specify whether you represent a large corporation with multiple sites or an SME with up to 10 locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify details" icon="check-circle">
    Review and confirm your profile information. This step enables access to the site assessment tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submit Your First Assessment

Once your profile is active, connect your commercial roof locations.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Large Corporations" icon="building-2">
    Enter details for multiple sites in a single request. Include site IDs, roof sizes, and location coordinates for network-wide analysis.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="SMEs" icon="home">
    Submit individual locations one at a time. Focus on up to 10 sites with standard roof specifications.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Review Energy Projections

After submission, Vantum generates initial savings estimates. You can view projected annual energy output in kWh and potential cost reductions.
