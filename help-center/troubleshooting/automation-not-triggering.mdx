---
title: Automation Not Triggering
description: Fix automations that are not running.
---

## Issue

- Your automation is not executing on expected events
- No alerts or actions triggered

## Possible Causes

- Trigger conditions not met
- Automation is paused
- Input data sync delay

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check activation status">
    Ensure your automation status is **Active** in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify triggers">
    Confirm trigger conditions match your current data/events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-sync data">
    Wait a few minutes or click **Sync Now** on the Data page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
