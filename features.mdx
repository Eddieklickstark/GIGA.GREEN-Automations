## Overview

Vantum automates the full lifecycle of solar energy projects. You manage site assessments, contracts, live monitoring, and financial reporting from a single dashboard. Each feature integrates directly with your existing ERP and utility systems.

<Callout kind="info">
  All features require an active Vantum subscription and valid site credentials.
</Callout>

## Automated Site Assessment

You upload roof plans or coordinates and receive a full feasibility report within minutes. The engine calculates irradiance, structural load, and grid connection viability.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Data" icon="upload">
    Provide site address and roof dimensions through the API or web form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run Analysis" icon="cpu">
    Vantum processes weather data and local regulations automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Report" icon="check-circle">
    Download the PDF or integrate results directly into your CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contract Management

You create, sign, and monitor lease agreements without leaving the platform. Status updates flow to all parties in real time.

````jsx
const contract = await vantum.contracts.create({
  siteId: "site_8f4k2p",
  lessee: "Acme Corp",
  termYears: 20,
  annualRate: 0.082
});
console.log(contract.status); // "pending_signature"
````
